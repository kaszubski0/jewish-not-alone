Addiction and recovery

Support exists inside the Jewish community — for the person struggling, and for the people who love them.

Recovery organizations

Recovery · Individuals and families

JACS

Jewish Alcoholics, Chemically Dependent Persons, and Significant Others — a program of the Jewish Board

  • All ages

A Jewish support network for people and families affected by addiction, run by the Jewish Board in New York. Like other 12-Step programs, it offers a safe, anonymous space, and it draws on Jewish values and traditions. Everyone is welcome, whatever their background or how they practice.

JACS connects people to meetings, programs, and recovery resources. Its Warm Line is staffed by volunteers who are themselves Jewish 12-Steppers.

Recovery · Online

Our Jewish Recovery

Online community

  • All ages

An online community for everyone in the Jewish community affected by addiction: people in recovery and the people who love them. It draws on Jewish values and recovery wisdom, and it began as a Facebook group in 2019.

Recovery · Treatment

Beit T'Shuvah

Los Angeles

  • All ages

A licensed addiction treatment center in Los Angeles that is also a congregation and an educational institute. It offers residential treatment, an intensive outpatient program, a family program, and help with gambling. It also hosts recovery meetings and Shabbat services, with an online archive of livestreamed services.
