Find a Jewish Family Service near you

Network of Jewish Human Service Agencies — Member Locator

  • All ages

Local Jewish Family Service (JFS) and Jewish Family & Children's Service (JFCS) agencies help people of every age: individuals, couples, families, and older adults. The Network of Jewish Human Service Agencies connects more than 170 of them across the United States and Canada.

The Network does not provide services itself. Its Member Locator lets you search by state or distance to find the agency nearest you.

For specific ages

Teens and young adults

BeWell

Jewish Federations of North America

  • Free
  • Ages 12–26

The mental health and wellbeing initiative of the Jewish Federations of North America, focused on young people aged 12 to 26. It offers resources and events for teens and young adults, parents and caregivers, and the Jewish professionals who work with them. BaMidbar and The Blue Dove Foundation are now part of BeWell.

Key facts: BeWell
CostFree resources
Who it's forAges 12–26
How to reach them
  • BeWell (opens in a new tab)

College and graduate students

Hillel

Hillel International

  • Free
  • Students

Jewish community and support on hundreds of campuses. Your Hillel staff know what support is available at your school. Working with the Network of Jewish Human Service Agencies and the Jewish Federations, Hillel connects students to services including mental health counseling.
