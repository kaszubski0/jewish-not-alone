Money, food, and work

Help with bills, food, and finding work is part of what local Jewish agencies do every day.

Where to start

Find a Jewish Family Service near you

Network of Jewish Human Service Agencies — Member Locator

  • All ages

Local Jewish Family Service (JFS) and Jewish Family & Children's Service (JFCS) agencies help people of every age: individuals, couples, families, and older adults. The Network of Jewish Human Service Agencies connects more than 170 of them across the United States and Canada.

The Network does not provide services itself. Its Member Locator lets you search by state or distance to find the agency nearest you.

Your local Jewish Federation

Jewish Federations of North America — Federation Finder

  • All ages

Local Jewish Federations fund and coordinate community services in their area, including the local Jewish Family Service. If you are not sure where to begin, your Federation can point you to the right local service.

What to ask when you call

  • Do you offer financial assistance, food, or job help?
  • What information should I have ready?
  • If you can’t help with this, who can?

Services differ from place to place

Each agency decides what it offers. A few questions will tell you quickly what is available near you.

If the first place you call can’t help, ask who can. Agencies refer people to each other all the time.

More about finding a local agency

Not sure where to start? One local agency can often help with all of this.
