About this guide

An independent, free guide to Jewish organizations in the United States.

What this site is

Jewish, Not Alone is a free, independent directory. It helps Jewish people in the United States find Jewish organizations to turn to: for reporting antisemitism, counseling, financial hardship, addiction recovery, help for older adults and Holocaust survivors, and support for students. Every organization listed is a Jewish organization.

What it is not

  • It is not a reporting channel. Reports go directly to each organization through its own official website, email, or phone line.
  • It is not affiliated with any organization listed, and no organization pays to be included.
  • It does not give legal, medical, or financial advice.

Privacy

There are no analytics, cookies, trackers, ads, or third-party scripts or fonts. Pages make no network requests after they load, and nothing you type is sent anywhere. The incident notes page may keep a draft in your own browser on your own device. You can remove it at any time with the Clear button.

When you follow a link to an organization, you leave this site. That organization’s own privacy policy applies.

How it’s kept accurate

Every entry was checked against the organization’s own official website and shows the date it was last verified. An automated check visits every link each week and flags any that stop working, so nobody is sent to a dead end.

Suggest a correction

If something is out of date or wrong, please tell us: open an issue on GitHub (opens in a new tab). Corrections about an organization’s services are best confirmed with that organization directly.

Coverage

The guide currently covers the United States. Other countries are planned.
