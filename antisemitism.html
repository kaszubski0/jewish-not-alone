Reporting antisemitism

Harassment, threats, vandalism, discrimination — on campus, at work, online, or anywhere else.

You can report even if it wasn’t a crime. Jewish organizations take reports about harassment, slurs, graffiti, and online abuse — not only criminal incidents. Small reports help them see patterns and protect others.

Reporting · Safety

SCN

Secure Community Network

  • Free
  • Anonymous OK
  • Non-criminal incidents accepted
  • All ages

The official safety and security organization of the Jewish community in North America. Its incident form covers threats, assaults, suspicious people or activity, vandalism and property damage, threatening calls, texts or social media posts, and antisemitic flyers.

The form has an option to stay anonymous. Reports go to SCN's Duty Desk and to your local Federation security director.

Takes non-criminal incidentsYes
Anonymous reportsYes
CostFree
How to reach them
  • Report an incident online (opens in a new tab)

Reporting · Legal help

ADL

Anti-Defamation League

  • Free
  • Non-criminal incidents accepted
  • All ages

Takes reports of antisemitism, bias, and discrimination, including harassment that is not a crime. You can use the online form, or a guided assistant that walks you through it and suggests resources.

ADL's Legal Action Network, run with Gibson Dunn and partner law firms, reviews whether free legal support can be offered.

Key facts: ADL
Takes non-criminal incidentsYes
Anonymous reportsNot stated — ask them
CostFree
How to reach them
  • Incident report form (opens in a new tab)
  • ADL Legal Action Network (opens in a new tab)
HoursOnline form, any time
What happens nextADL's experts review the report to see how ADL may be able to help. It may also be counted in ADL's annual Audit of Antisemitic Incidents.

Reporting · Campus

ReportCampusHate.org

A partnership of Hillel International, ADL, and the Secure Community Network

  • Free
  • Anonymous OK
  • Non-criminal incidents accepted
  • Students

For antisemitic or bias incidents targeting Jewish students, faculty, and staff at colleges and universities in North America. You can report anonymously, but leaving contact details lets them help you more directly.

Key facts: ReportCampusHate.org

Anonymous reportsYes
CostFree
How to reach them

Legal help · Campus and K–12

CALL

Campus Antisemitism Legal Line

  • Free
  • Non-criminal incidents accepted
  • Students

A free, confidential way to report antisemitism on campus and get legal support. It is run by Hillel International, ADL, and the Louis D. Brandeis Center. Examples include being treated differently because you are Jewish, harassment in person or online, and damage to your property.

There is a separate route for K–12 schools.

Key facts: CALL
Takes non-criminal incidentsYes
Anonymous reportsNot stated — ask them
CostFree and confidential
How to reach them
  • Request legal review (college) (opens in a new tab)
  • K–12 schools (opens in a new tab)
  • CALL@legal-protection.org
HoursOnline form, any time
What happens nextAttorneys from Hillel, ADL, and the Brandeis Center review each report together and decide on next steps.
