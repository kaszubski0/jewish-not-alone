Agencies like these commonly help with

  • Counseling and mental health
  • Financial assistance and food
  • Career and employment
  • Youth and family programs
  • Older adults and Holocaust survivors
  • Immigrants and refugees
  • People with disabilities and caregivers

One network, many local agencies

The Network of Jewish Human Service Agencies (NJHSA) brings together more than 170 Jewish human service organizations across the United States and Canada. They serve people of every age.

The Network doesn’t provide services directly. Its Member Locator finds the local agency that does. What each agency offers varies, so ask what is available near you.

Find a Jewish Family Service near you

Network of Jewish Human Service Agencies — Member Locator

  • All ages

Local Jewish Family Service (JFS) and Jewish Family & Children's Service (JFCS) agencies help people of every age: individuals, couples, families, and older adults. The Network of Jewish Human Service Agencies connects more than 170 of them across the United States and Canada.

The Network does not provide services itself. Its Member Locator lets you search by state or distance to find the agency nearest you.
