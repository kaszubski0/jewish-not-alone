Common questions

Short answers to the things people often wonder before reaching out.

It wasn’t a crime. Can I still report it?

Yes. ADL and the Secure Community Network both accept incidents that are not crimes, such as harassment, slurs, flyers, and online abuse. See who takes reports.

It feels too small to report.

Organizations use reports to see patterns. A small report can help protect someone else, and it helps make sure what happened to you is counted.

Will my name become public?

The Secure Community Network and ReportCampusHate.org let you report anonymously. Before you share details with any organization, ask how they handle confidentiality.

I’m afraid of retaliation.

That is a real and reasonable fear, and it is why anonymous reporting exists. You can also talk it through with a counselor first. Find counseling and support.

What happens after I report?

It depends on the organization. SCN analysts assess each report and refer credible threats as needed. ADL reviews each report to see how it may be able to help. Each listing on this site has a line called “What happens next.”

Do I have to report to the police too?

That is your decision. The organizations listed here are separate from the police.

I’m not a student and not a teenager. Is there anything for me?

Yes. Local Jewish Family Service agencies serve people of every age. Find a Jewish agency near you.

I don’t want to report anything. I just want to talk.

That is completely fine. Find counseling and emotional support.

Does this site keep what I type?

No. There are no analytics, cookies, or trackers, and nothing is sent anywhere. The notes page can keep a draft in your own browser so you don’t lose work. It has a Clear button. More about privacy.

Not sure where to start? One local agency can often help with all of this.
